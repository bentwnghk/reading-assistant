1. Introduction & Welcome

What Mr.🆖 ProReader is, who this guide is for, and how to install the app.

Welcome to Mr.🆖 ProReader

Mr.🆖 ProReader is an AI-powered English reading assistant designed for classrooms and self-study. Feed it a photo of a page, a PDF, a text picked from your school's text repository — or, if you have nothing to hand, let the AI Text Generator write one for you. From that single source the platform produces a complete, age-calibrated lesson in minutes.

Every reading session is a full learning unit, organised into six stages:

  • Before You Read — a pre-reading panel that activates prior knowledge, sets a purpose for reading, pre-teaches the few words that would otherwise block comprehension, supplies essential background, and invites the reader to make a prediction — all without revealing the text's content.
  • Understand — an AI summary; Original / Adapted / Simplified versions of the text you can flip between, with a Read Along mode that highlights each sentence in sync with text-to-speech (tap any sentence to jump); a mind map; an AI-generated image visualization; multi-metric readability scores; word-level CEFR difficulty highlighting (A1–C2) overlaid on the passage, plus on-demand sentence analysis; a single-word vocabulary glossary with definitions, syllabification, examples and pronunciation; and a separate Phrases & Collocations panel that pulls out multi-word expressions (phrasal verbs, idioms, delexicalized-verb collocations) with notes on common Cantonese/Chinese-L1 transfer mistakes.
  • Practice — spaced-repetition flashcards; vocabulary quizzes; solo spelling games (listen-type, scramble, and fill-blanks modes) and real-time multiplayer Spelling Battles against classmates; a multi-type comprehension reading test (multiple choice, true/false/not given, short answer, inference, vocab-in-context, referencing); and a full grammar module with four tabs — extracted Topics (tenses, conditionals, passive voice, relative clauses, etc.), enriched Lessons (forms, signal words, common mistakes, concept-checking questions, AI-graded practice, topic-specific text highlighting), a timed Quiz, and five grammar Games (Roulette, Error Surgery, Workshop, Duel, Word Scramble).
  • AI Tutor — a chat-based reading tutor that grounds every answer in your text. Upload a photo of a comprehension question and choose Hint me, Step-by-step, or Give answer.
  • Master — a skill profile built across every session that powers adaptive targeted practice drills; leaderboards and achievements; and a cross-session My Vocabulary hub (single words and multi-word phrases) with mastery tracking and spaced-repetition review.
  • For classrooms — a shared text repository, session sharing & assignments, vocabulary and review-list sharing, one-click Excel exports, and live class dashboards.
One source, one lesson Whatever you feed in, every output above is generated from the same source text and calibrated to the reader's age — so a 9-year-old's summary, glossary and games all land simpler than a 16-year-old's.

It works in the browser and can be installed as a mobile / desktop app (a Progressive Web App, PWA). Sign-in uses a Google account. Under the hood, every AI feature can be powered by your personal or school subscription, your own API key, or a free access password — you choose.

5. Vocabulary Tools & Spaced Repetition

The in-session Glossary hub: words, phrases, flashcards, spelling game, quiz, and phrases & collocations — all powered by the Leitner SRS algorithm.

Once you have highlighted words (from AI Vocabulary Suggest or by selecting text), the Glossary section can generate definitions. It then becomes a hub with five sub-tabs: Words, Phrases, Flashcard, Spelling, and Quiz. The Words and Phrases tabs split your glossary by entry type — single words versus multi-word chunks (phrasal verbs, collocations, compound nouns) — so you can study each set separately. Directly below the Glossary, the Phrases & Collocations section extracts additional high-value multi-word expressions with Cantonese-L1 contrast notes. Every word and phrase you practice is tracked by a spaced-repetition system (SRS) so you review the right items at the right time.

C. Troubleshooting / FAQ

ProblemLikely cause & fix
AI features do nothing / errorNo Billing Mode set, or key/password missing. Open Settings → General and complete one of the three setup modes (Chapter 2).
"Limit reached" on image generationDaily visualization quota hit. Free = 1/day, Subscription = 2/day. Resets daily, or switch to Meter mode for unlimited.
Signed out unexpectedlyThe idle timeout (default 90 min) or the 3-day session ceiling was reached. A 1-minute warning toast precedes idle sign-out.

Shared session missing my answersExpected — sharing strips answers/scores/chat to give recipients a clean copy. Your original is untouched.
