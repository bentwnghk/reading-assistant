# Mr.🆖 ProReader — FAQs

## Q1: I am reading a text which I find really difficult to understand. How can Mr.🆖 ProReader help me?

Mr.🆖 ProReader is built exactly for moments like that. Just upload your text (or snap a photo of it), and the AI goes to work in several ways:

1. **Instant Summary & Mind Map** — Get a clear TL;DR, key points, and a visual mind map so you grasp the big picture before diving into the details.

2. **Simplified & Adapted Text** — The app rewrites the passage at your level using simpler vocabulary and shorter sentences, while keeping the original meaning intact.

3. **Vocabulary Glossary** — Every tricky word is highlighted and explained with bilingual definitions, syllable breakdowns, and example sentences. You can review them later with flashcards, spelling games, and quizzes powered by spaced repetition.

4. **Grammar Analysis** — Complex grammar structures are identified and explained with patterns and examples from the text itself, plus interactive grammar games to reinforce your understanding.

5. **AI Tutor** — A conversational AI tutor is always on standby. Ask it anything about the passage — highlight a confusing sentence, and it will break it down for you step by step.

6. **Reading Comprehension Test** — Once you feel ready, take a generated quiz covering main ideas, details, inferences, and more, with detailed explanations for every answer.

Think of Mr.🆖 ProReader as a patient, personalized tutor that turns any difficult text into a learnable experience.

## Q2: If there is a word or a phrase that I don't understand in the text, what can I do?

You have two great options, each designed for a different learning moment:

### Option 1: Highlight the word, add it, then tap to see its Glossary entry

Here's how it works in three simple steps:

1. **Select (highlight)** any word or phrase in the text. A small toolbar pops up right above your selection.
2. Tap **"+ Add Word"** on the toolbar to mark it. The word gets highlighted in yellow throughout the text.
3. Once the AI has generated the **Glossary** for your text, any of your highlighted words that have a glossary entry will show a small amber indicator dot. **Tap the highlighted word** and a popover instantly appears with its bilingual definition (English + Chinese), syllable breakdown, part of speech, and an example sentence.

It's fast, visual, and perfect for quick lookups while you're in the flow of reading. Every word you add is also automatically saved to your personal vocabulary bank (**My Vocabulary**) for later review with flashcards, spelling games, and quizzes.

### Option 2: Highlight the word and ask the AI Tutor

Select (highlight) the word or phrase, then switch to the **AI Tutor** chat. The tutor already knows your text, so it will give you a deeper, more conversational explanation — not just a dictionary definition, but *why* that word or phrase is used in context, how it connects to the passage's meaning, and even nuances like tone or connotation. You can ask follow-up questions too, like "Can you give me another example?" or "Is there a simpler word for this?"

### So what's the difference?

| | **Glossary (Add Word → Tap)** | **AI Tutor (Highlight → Ask)** |
|---|---|---|
| **Speed** | Instant pop-up after glossary is generated | Takes a moment for AI to respond |
| **Depth** | Concise dictionary-style entry | In-depth, contextual explanation |
| **Interactivity** | One-way info display | Two-way conversation — ask follow-ups |
| **Feeds vocabulary review** | Yes — saved to My Vocabulary automatically | No — it's a free-form chat for understanding |
| **Best for** | Quick meaning checks during reading | When you want to truly *understand* a word or phrase in context |

**Pro tip:** For the best experience, combine both — use Option 1 to build your vocabulary list as you read, and turn to the AI Tutor (Option 2) whenever you need a deeper explanation. The words you save through Option 1 are the ones that get reinforced later through flashcards, spelling games, and quizzes with spaced repetition.

## Q3: When I read a text, sometimes I feel like I know most of the words in a sentence, but I still don't understand what the sentence is saying. What can I do?

That's a really common experience — understanding individual words but missing the meaning of the whole sentence is usually about sentence structure, grammar, or idiomatic usage. Mr.🆖 ProReader has **three** powerful tools to help:

### 1. Sentence Analysis — Break it down structurally

Select (highlight) the confusing sentence in your text, and tap the **brain icon** on the selection toolbar. The AI will analyze the sentence and produce a detailed breakdown in five sections:

- **句意概要 (Sentence Summary)** — What the sentence means in simple terms
- **詞彙分析 (Vocabulary Analysis)** — Identifies 2–4 challenging words with bilingual definitions and example sentences
- **句法分析 (Syntactic Analysis)** — Breaks down the sentence type (simple/compound/complex), main clause components (subject, verb, object), and explains tricky grammatical structures like passive voice, inverted word order, or relative clauses
- **語用分析 (Pragmatic Analysis)** — Explains the sentence's function in context (describing, arguing, contrasting, etc.), any figures of speech (metaphor, simile, personification, hyperbole), and the tone (formal/informal, factual/emotional)
- **學習建議 (Learning Tips)** — 2–3 practical, age-appropriate tips for understanding similar sentences in the future

The analyzed sentence gets underlined in blue in your text, so you can tap it anytime to revisit the explanation.

### 2. AI Tutor — Get a conversational explanation

Highlight the sentence and take it to the **AI Tutor** chat. Because the tutor already knows your entire text, it can explain the sentence *in context* — not just what it means in isolation, but how it fits into the paragraph and why the author phrased it that way. You can keep asking follow-up questions until it fully clicks, like:

- "Can you rephrase this in simpler words?"
- "What does 'X' refer to in this sentence?"
- "Why is this sentence important in the paragraph?"

### 3. Grammar Analysis — Understand the underlying patterns

If confusing sentences keep popping up, it might be a grammar pattern you haven't mastered yet. Run the **Grammar Analysis** on your text — it will scan for grammar topics (tenses, conditionals, passive voice, etc.) and provide explanations with patterns and examples drawn directly from your text. You can even reinforce your understanding with **Grammar Games** like Word Scramble, Error Surgery, and Grammar Roulette.

**Pro tip:** Start with Sentence Analysis for a quick, structured breakdown. If the sentence is part of a bigger confusion, bring it to the AI Tutor for a richer discussion. And if you notice a pattern — like always struggling with passive voice or conditional sentences — head to Grammar Analysis to tackle the root cause.

## Q4: I know that vocabulary building is key to improving my reading ability, but it is really difficult and boring to remember so many new and unfamiliar words.

We totally get it — rote memorization is a drag. That's exactly why Mr.🆖 ProReader turns vocabulary building into something that feels less like studying and more like playing. Here's how it keeps you engaged while actually helping you *remember*:

### 1. Vocabulary comes to you naturally — no flashcard deck to build

As you read texts in Mr.🆖 ProReader, you're already encountering new words. Simply highlight and add them, and the AI automatically generates bilingual definitions, syllable breakdowns, and example sentences for each one. No manual data entry — your vocabulary bank grows organically as you read.

### 2. Flashcards with Spaced Repetition — review smarter, not harder

The built-in **flashcard system** uses a scientifically-backed spaced repetition algorithm (Leitner 5-box system). Instead of reviewing every word equally, it prioritizes the words you struggle with. Rate each card with **Again**, **Hard**, **Good**, or **Easy** — and the algorithm schedules the next review accordingly (immediately, 1 day, 3 days, 7 days, 14 days, or 30 days later). Over time, you spend less time on easy words and more on the ones that need reinforcement.

### 3. Spelling Games — learn by doing, not by staring

Four interactive spelling game modes make active recall feel like play:

| Mode | How it works |
|------|-------------|
| **Listen & Type** | Hear the word, then spell it out |
| **Scramble** | Unscramble the jumbled letters |
| **Fill in the Blanks** | Complete the word with missing letters |
| **Mixed** | A random mix of all three modes |

Each game lets you choose difficulty levels, word count, and even a **Time Challenge** mode for an extra adrenaline rush. You earn points, track streaks, and see your performance tier (Master / Great / Good / Keep Practicing) with animated visual effects.

### 4. Vocabulary Quiz — test yourself under pressure

Take auto-generated quizzes drawn from your own word list. Like the spelling games, quizzes support difficulty levels and Time Challenge mode, so you can push yourself as you improve.

### 5. My Vocabulary page — your personal word command center

Everything lives in one place: the **My Vocabulary** page (`/vocabulary`). Here you can:

- Browse all your words in a sortable, filterable **table**
- Jump straight into **flashcards**, **spelling games**, or **quizzes**
- Use **auto-select** strategies to pick the right words to review — overdue reviews, hard words, random picks, new words, or oldest reviewed
- Create named **review lists** (e.g., "DSE Vocabulary Set 1") and organize words into focused study groups
- **Export** your glossary to PDF, Word, CSV, or even as an image
- Track your **review history** with detailed session stats

### 6. Shared by your teacher — vocabulary assigned to you

If you're a student, your teacher can share vocabulary lists and review lists directly with you. When you accept a shared list, the words are added to your personal vocabulary bank automatically — complete with definitions and examples — so you can start reviewing right away.

**Pro tip:** Don't try to learn everything at once. After each reading session, spend just 5–10 minutes on flashcards or a quick spelling game. The spaced repetition system will handle the rest — showing you words right when you're about to forget them, which is scientifically the best time to reinforce memory. Small, consistent doses beat cramming every time.

## Q5: I often struggle with questions that appear in my school's reading exams, especially those DSE exam practices.

That's where Mr.🆖 ProReader's **Reading Comprehension Test** really shines. It simulates real exam conditions and targets the skills that DSE and school exams test. Here's how it helps:

### 1. Auto-generated, exam-style questions

Upload any text, and the AI generates **10 questions** tailored to your age and school level (Primary / Secondary / DSE). The question types mirror what you'll see in real exams:

| Question Type | What it tests |
|---|---|
| **Multiple Choice** | Understanding of details, main idea |
| **True / False / Not Given** | Whether a statement agrees with, contradicts, or isn't covered by the text |
| **Vocabulary in Context** | What a word means *in this specific passage* |
| **Referencing** | What a pronoun or phrase refers to in context |
| **Inference** | What can be logically deduced but isn't explicitly stated |
| **Short Answer** | Open-ended responses requiring 2–3 sentences |

For DSE students, the distribution is weighted heavily toward **inference** (3 questions) and **vocabulary in context** (2 questions) — exactly what DSE papers emphasize.

### 2. Detailed explanations with skill tracking

Every question is tagged with the skill it tests: **Main Idea**, **Detail**, **Inference**, **Vocabulary**, or **Author's Purpose**. After submitting, you get a skill-by-skill breakdown showing exactly where you're strong and where you need more work, plus detailed explanations for every answer in both English and Chinese.

### 3. Retry missed questions — and targeted practice

Got some wrong? Two powerful follow-up options:

- **Retry Missed** — Re-attempt only the questions you got wrong
- **Targeted Practice** — The AI generates a *brand new set of questions* focused specifically on your weakest skills. So if you struggled with inference, it creates new inference questions from the same text to give you more practice

**Pro tip:** After each reading session, generate a test and treat it like a real exam — don't look at the text while answering. Then use **Targeted Practice** to get fresh questions for your weakest skills. Repeat across different texts, and you'll see steady improvement in exactly the areas that matter most for your exams.

## Q6: I don't know what text to read. I just want to read more to practice for the DSE.

Great news — Mr.🆖 ProReader has you covered even before you start reading. Here are three ways to find practice texts:

### 1. Text Repository — Ready-made texts at your fingertips

When you open Mr.🆖 ProReader, you'll see the **Text Repository** — a built-in library of reading texts uploaded and shared by your teachers or school. You can browse by visibility level:

- **Class** — Texts shared within your specific class
- **School** — Texts available to everyone in your school
- **Public** — Texts shared across all schools

Just pick a text, tap "Load," and you're ready to go — no uploading needed. The repository is searchable, so you can quickly find texts on topics that interest you.

### 2. "Pick a text for me" — Can't decide? Let the app choose

Every time you open Mr.🆖 ProReader, a welcome dialog appears with a **"Read from Repository"** option. Tap it, and the app will randomly pick a text from the repository for you — complete with a preview of the title, opening lines, and who uploaded it. Not feeling that one? Hit **"Pick another"** and it'll suggest a different text. Keep shuffling until one catches your eye, then tap **"Start Reading"** and dive straight in.

### 3. Upload your own DSE practice materials

Found a DSE past paper, a newspaper article, or a workbook passage you want to practice with? You can upload it yourself:

- **Photos** — Snap a photo of a textbook page or worksheet (supports PNG, JPG, WEBP; multiple images for multi-page texts)
- **PDFs** — Upload a PDF and the app extracts the text automatically
- **Paste text** — Type or paste any text directly

The AI-powered **OCR** will extract the text, and then all of Mr.🆖 ProReader's features — summary, mind map, glossary, adapted text, reading test, and more — become available for that text.

**Pro tip:** The app already comes with all DSE past exam papers from 2012 onwards pre-loaded in the Text Repository. So you can jump right in — open the app, hit "Read from Repository," and pick a DSE past paper to practice with. Make it a habit: one past paper a day, work through the text with the glossary and adapted text for understanding, then take the Reading Test at the end to check your comprehension. Consistent daily practice with real exam material is one of the most effective ways to prepare.

## Q7: I'm working on a DSE past paper and am struggling with a very hard question that I don't know how to do. Can Mr.🆖 ProReader help me?

Absolutely — Mr.🆖 ProReader can help you tackle even the toughest DSE questions. Here's how:

### 1. Upload the past paper and let the AI break it down

If you haven't already, upload the DSE past paper (photo, PDF, or paste the text). The app will extract the text and generate a full suite of learning aids — summary, mind map, adapted text, glossary — so you have a solid understanding of the passage before you even look at the questions.

### 2. AI Tutor — your on-demand exam coach

Switch to the **AI Tutor** chat and you can:

- **Type or paste the exam question** directly into the chat, and the tutor will guide you through how to approach it — not just give you the answer, but help you *think* through it step by step
- **Highlight the part of the text** the question refers to, and ask the tutor how that section connects to the question
- Ask follow-up questions like:
  - "This question asks me to infer the author's attitude — how do I figure that out?"
  - "I don't understand why the answer is B and not D — can you explain the difference?"
  - "What clues in the text should I be looking at for this type of question?"

The tutor uses **Socratic questioning** — it guides you to discover the answer through thoughtful hints rather than just telling you. But if you're still stuck after 2–3 hints, it'll gently provide the answer with a clear explanation.

### 3. Upload an image of the question for direct help

You can also **snap a photo of the exam question** and upload it directly in the AI Tutor chat. The AI will read the question from the image and help you work through it based on the passage you're currently reading. Three levels of help are available:

- **"Hint me"** — The tutor gives you hints and guiding questions so you can find the answer yourself (always available)
- **"Step-by-step"** — A full walkthrough explaining how to arrive at the answer (requires **Jailbreak Mode** in Settings)
- **"Give Answer"** — The direct answer with a clear explanation (requires both **Jailbreak Mode** and **Show 'Give Answer'** in Settings)

To unlock the latter two, go to **Settings** and turn on **Jailbreak Mode**, then optionally enable **Show 'Give Answer'**.

**Pro tip:** When you encounter a hard DSE question, resist the urge to go straight to "Give Answer." Instead, upload the question image and start with "Hint me" — the tutor will guide you to find the answer yourself. That process of reasoning through it is exactly what builds the skills you need on exam day. Use "Step-by-step" or "Give Answer" only when you're truly stuck and need to see how it's done.

## Q8: Can I come back to a text or passage that I read earlier in Mr.🆖 ProReader, and on a different device?

Yes, absolutely. Mr.🆖 ProReader automatically saves your reading sessions and syncs them to the cloud, so you can pick up right where you left off — on any device.

### 1. Automatic cloud saving

Every reading session is automatically saved to your account, including the full text, glossary, highlighted words, flashcard progress, reading test results, grammar analysis, tutor chat history, and all other work you've done on that text. There's no "Save" button to worry about — it just happens.

### 2. Resume from any device

Sign in with the same Google account on any device — phone, tablet, or computer. To find your past sessions:

1. Tap the **bar chart icon** in the header
2. This opens the **"Learning Journey"** dialog
3. Switch to the **"Sessions"** tab (with the clock icon)
4. All your past reading sessions are listed there — simply tap on any session to reload it with everything intact, exactly as you left it

Your settings and preferences also sync across devices.

### 3. Session management

From the **"Sessions"** tab in **Learning Journey**, you can also:

- **Load** a previous session to continue working on it
- **Export** sessions for backup (download as a file)
- **Delete** sessions you no longer need
- **Share** sessions with others

**Pro tip:** This makes it easy to start reading on your phone during a commute, then pick up the same text on your laptop when you get home. Everything — including your tutor chat, vocabulary progress, and test scores — travels with you. Just make sure you're signed in with the same Google account on all your devices.

## Q9: Can I track and see my learning progress and history?

Yes! Mr.🆖 ProReader makes it easy to see the big picture of your learning journey and dive into the details.

### 1. Learning Journey — your personal dashboard

Tap the **bar chart icon** in the header to open **"Learning Journey"**. It has two tabs:

- **"Overview"** — A visual analytics dashboard showing your cumulative stats: total reading texts, summaries generated, mind maps created, adapted/simplified texts used, sentences analyzed, vocabulary collected, AI Tutor questions asked, and average learning progress. You'll also see score trends for Reading Tests and Vocabulary Quizzes, and a breakdown of which AI features you've used most.

- **"Sessions"** — A chronological list of all your past reading sessions with details like vocabulary count, test scores, and learning progress for each one.

### 2. Achievements — unlock badges as you learn

As you read and learn, you'll automatically earn **achievement badges** across 12 different types — from completing reading sessions to acing vocabulary quizzes. Each badge tracks your milestones and motivates you to keep going.

### 3. Leaderboard — see how you stack up

Check the **Leaderboard** to compare your reading activity, test scores, and vocabulary growth with classmates or other students. A little friendly competition can be a great motivator.

### 4. My Vocabulary — track your word mastery

The **My Vocabulary** page (`/vocabulary`) shows every word you've ever encountered, along with mastery levels (0–5), review counts, accuracy rates, and SRS scheduling status. You can see exactly which words you've mastered and which ones still need work.

**Pro tip:** Make it a habit to check the **"Overview"** tab in Learning Journey once a week. Seeing your stats grow over time — more texts read, more vocabulary collected, higher test scores — is a powerful motivator. And if you notice your Reading Test scores plateauing on a particular skill, that's your signal to focus targeted practice on that area.

## Q10: I'm a teacher / school administrator. What additional features are available for me?

Mr.🆖 ProReader has a full set of management and monitoring tools designed specifically for teachers and school administrators. Here's what's available to you:

### For Teachers

**1. Teacher Dashboard — monitor your classes**

Open the **"Teacher Dashboard"** (accessible from the header menu) to see real-time analytics for your classes:

- **Daily Learning Activity** — charts showing reading texts, vocabulary growth, and average learning progress over time
- **AI Features Usage** — see how students are using summaries, mind maps, adapted text, glossary, grammar analysis, flashcards, and more
- **Score Tracking** — Reading Test scores, Spelling scores, Quiz scores, Grammar Quiz & Game scores
- Filter by class and time period (1W to 1Y)

**2. Student Data — drill into individual students**

View detailed reading session data for each student, including learning progress, test scores, vocabulary count, and more. Filter by class and date range. **Export to Excel** for record-keeping or parent meetings.

**3. AI Questions Tracker — see what students are asking**

Track every question your students ask the AI Tutor, including frequency, which students asked, and when. Great for identifying common areas of confusion so you can address them in class.

**4. Text Repository — upload and share reading materials**

Upload reading texts (images, PDFs, or paste text) and control who sees them:

- **Class-only** — visible only to students in a specific class
- **School** — visible to everyone in your school
- **Public** — visible to all schools

Edit, rename, or delete texts you've uploaded. Import/export repository texts as JSON files.

**5. Share vocabulary and review lists with students**

Select words from your own vocabulary and share them directly with students in your school. You can also create named **review lists** (e.g., "DSE Set 1") and share entire lists — students receive a notification and can accept the list with one tap.

**6. Manage classes and students**

Create classes, assign students, and edit class details. Add or remove students from your classes at any time.

### For School Administrators

Everything above, plus:

**7. User Management**

Manage users within your school and assign teacher/student roles.

**8. School Subscriptions**

Purchase and manage a subscription for your entire school. Allocate seats, monitor usage, and revoke access when needed. Students covered by a school subscription don't need individual subscriptions.

**Pro tip:** Use the **AI Questions Tracker** to discover common misconceptions across your class. If you notice multiple students asking about the same concept, that's a clear signal to address it in your next lesson. Combined with the **Student Data** tab and Excel exports, you'll have everything you need for data-driven teaching — without any extra administrative overhead.
